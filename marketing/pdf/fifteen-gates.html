Why this exists

What ships when nobody checks

Every content site that grows past a few hundred pages accumulates the same debt. None of it fails a build. All of it fails a reader, and answer engines stop citing a site long before a person complains.

  1. A currency sign that renders as a question mark in the search snippet.Pasted from a spreadsheet. Fine in the editor, wrong on the results page.
  2. A title cut on the last stop word.A template added a brand suffix and truncated at 60 characters. 361 titles ended on "the" or "for".
  3. The same paragraph on 121 pages with the numbers swapped.Exact-match dedupe never saw it. Google's spam policy calls it scaled content abuse.
  4. An H2 with nothing under it.Twelve headings generated, nine filled. The other three rendered as a promise with no delivery.
  5. An anchor that names one district and links to another.The reader clicks "Playa Norte overview" and lands in Playa Sur.
  6. A page merged away six weeks ago, still advertised in llms.txt.Answer engines read the index instead of crawling. They cited a redirect.
  7. "7.5% net yield" that nobody ever sourced.On 70 pages. Buyers who did the maths did not come back.

How to read this document. Pages 3 to 6 walk through the fifteen gates in four groups: mechanical, corpus debt, links and routing, trust. Page 7 shows how to run them. Page 8 shows what to do when they find more than you can fix.

Fifteen gates2
Group one

Mechanical: minutes to fix

Deterministic checks. Fix these first; they take minutes and they hide the real findings behind noise.

01

Characters

fail

FailureCurrency signs in tables, emoji in headings, em dashes auto-corrected on every line.

bad-characters.mdx:8 currency sign ₡: spell it CRC bad-characters.mdx:8 decorative pictograph 🏝 bad-characters.mdx:8 em or en dash: use a comma, a colon, parentheses or a hyphen in ranges

Fixgates --fix rewrites signs to codes and removes pictographs. Dashes are left to a person: the right replacement depends on the sentence.

02

MDX compile

fail

FailureAn eleven-minute build fails on file 1,214 because of a quote inside a JSX string. Fix, push, wait, file 1,380.

bad-mdx.mdx Expected a closing tag for `<Callout>` (10:1-10:22)

FixEvery file is compiled up front with the build's own parser. All failures at once, with the line.

03

Frontmatter

fail

FailureMissing fields, cut titles, descriptions ending in an ellipsis, hero images on a stock host that changed its hotlink policy, related slugs pointing at pages that no longer exist.

bad-frontmatter.mdx title length 17, expected 40 to 60 bad-frontmatter.mdx description ends on a stop word (cut) bad-frontmatter.mdx relatedSlug "ghost-slug" does not exist

FixRewrite the field. For cut titles, drop the brand suffix rather than the promise.

04

Structure

fail

FailureA commercial page that reads well and answers nothing: no answer at the top, one table, two H2s, no risks, four numbers in two thousand words. Nothing to quote, nothing to decide with.

bad-structure.mdx missing answer-first block (Quick answer or TL;DR) bad-structure.mdx heading level skip H2 to H4 at line 12 bad-structure.mdx draft or source marker left in text ([VERIFY], TODO, source needed)

FixAnswer-first block, four H2s, six table rows, the FAQ count the collection needs, pros and cons, risks, scenarios, three numeric facts per 500 words, five internal links. Thresholds live in the config.

Fifteen gates3
Group two

Corpus debt: where the work is

These three gates find what generated content leaves behind. They are the reason the package exists.

05

Duplication

fail

Failure121 project cards with the same closing paragraph and different numbers. The exact-match checker passed every one of them.

2 pages share: "the first 50 metres from the high tide line belong to the state and cannot be sold…" 2 pages share a template with swapped numbers: "median asking price per sqm in # is # crc, based on the registry…"

HowEvery paragraph of twelve words or more, compared exactly and with the digits masked, across pages and within a page. Same-shaped tables are structure, not prose, and are not counted. Disclaimers that must repeat go into a boilerplate list.

FixOne page keeps the paragraph, the others link to it. For templates with swapped numbers, write the sentence from the fact, not from the template.

06

Shared sentences

fail

FailureThe block check passed. The corpus still read as machine-written: one sentence, inside different paragraphs, on 454 pages.

3x sentence: "every lease longer than three years must be registered at the land registry within 30 days"

HowSentences of ten words or more, list items and table cells of eight words or more, normalised and counted across indexable pages. Three pages is a finding.

FixSame as gate 05, one level down.

07

Hollow sections

fail

FailureAn outline with twelve H2s, nine written. Sections that open by restating their heading. Eleven FAQ answers that all say "Yes, in most cases. Contact our team."

hollow.mdx empty section "What the charge covers" hollow.mdx section "How the charge is set" holds only shared filler hollow.mdx numbered list or steps start above 1 hollow.mdx 1 table cell(s) holding only a comma

FixWrite the section or delete the heading. A heading that promises and delivers nothing costs more than no heading.

Fifteen gates4
Group three

Links and routing: usually one config change

Five gates that fail quietly between deploys and are cheap to keep green once the pipeline generates the right files.

08

Internal links

fail

FailureTwo content waves merged on separate branches. Neither had a broken link. The merged site had forty.

/guides/does-not-exist/ (1 page(s), e.g. index.html)

HowEvery href, src and action in the built HTML resolved against the build and the redirects file. Without a build, source links are checked against the content index.

09

Link semantics

warn

FailureAn anchor names one district, the href goes to another. A project name links to a generic guide instead of the project's page. One pillar linked four times on one screen.

bad-links.mdx anchor names playa norte but link goes to playa sur: [Playa Norte overview](/areas/playa-sur/) bad-links.mdx anchor is an entity name but links elsewhere: [Marina Towers](/guides/clean-guide/)

FixMake the anchor say where the link goes. Entities link to their own page.

10

Meta

warn

Failure"Condo Guide 2025 vs 2026: Buyer Guide". The year twice, the format word twice, forty per cent of the title spent on nothing.

bad-meta.mdx title repeats the year · title repeats "guide"
13

Redirects

fail

FailureA merged into B, then B into C. Search engines follow two hops grudgingly and three not at all. A page that still existed was redirected away.

cycle: /old-a -> /old-b -> /old-a chain of 3: /x1 -> /x2 -> /x3 (point the first source at the final target) live page /guides/bad-meta/ is redirected away
14

AI index

fail

Failurellms.txt generated once. A month later: four pages merged away, one set to noindex, two collections added. Answer engines kept reading the old file.

index lists a page that does not exist: /guides/ghost-page/ index lists noindex page /guides/draft-page/ indexable page missing from the index: /guides/bad-structure/

FixGenerate the index from the corpus in the build. Run the gate after the build.

Fifteen gates5
Group four

Trust: editorial work

The last three gates are about whether a person, a quality rater or an answer engine should believe the page.

11

Readability

warn

FailureA page that passed every structural gate and still read as generated: a dash every second line, four "Scenario A to D" blocks, sixty-word sentences.

bad-readability.mdx 5 sentences over 45 words

HowDashes per 500 words, template scenario blocks, corpus stamps left in text, sentences over 45 words, paragraphs over 170 words. Warns rather than fails: a legal page may legitimately run long.

12

Images

fail

FailureA CDN account migrated. 2,300 hero images returned 404 for nine days. The pages still built.

bad-images.mdx local image missing in public: /images/missing-hero.jpg

HowEvery image URL under src/ gets a HEAD request, twelve in parallel with one retry. Local paths are checked against public/. Run it on a schedule: images break between deploys.

15

Unsourced claims

fail

Failure"A coastal one-bedroom earns a 7.5% net yield with 82% occupancy." No source, no method, on 70 pages.

bad-claims.mdx yield or ROI figure without a source: "7.5% net yield" bad-claims.mdx nightly rate without a source: "$140 per night"

HowYield, ROI, occupancy, nightly rate, income per period and appreciation figures in every paragraph. A paragraph passes when it names an allowed source, uses a sourcing phrase, or frames the figure as a worked example.

FixName the source in the same paragraph, turn the figure into an example with stated assumptions, or delete it. This is the gate that takes days, and the one that decides whether the site is believed.

Fix in order. Gates 01 to 04 take minutes. Gates 05 to 07 hold the corpus debt. Gates 08 to 14 are usually one change in the pipeline. Gate 15 is editorial work.

Run it

Three steps, no setup ceremony

  1. Install. Node 20 or newer, any folder of MDX or Markdown with frontmatter (Astro content collections work out of the box).
    npm i -D @operstack/gates
  2. Describe the site. One JSON file at the project root. Or skip it: the tool detects src/content/* and applies default thresholds.
  3. Run. npx gates. Terminal table, Markdown report in .gates/, JSON for CI. Exit code 1 when something must not ship.

See every gate fire first. npx @operstack/gates --demo runs the fictional Isla Verde site that ships with the package: one deliberate defect per gate and one page that passes all fifteen.

Flags. --only 1,4,15, --fix, --offline, --fail-on warn, --json.

{
  "siteUrl": "https://example.com",
  "contentDir": "src/content",
  "collections": {
    "guides":   { "url": "/guides/",
                  "minWords": 2000, "faq": 5,
                  "commercial": true },

                  "minWords": 400,

Tell the model before the gate tells you. The package includes a rules file for Cursor and Claude Code (rules/content-gates.md). It states every rule the gates enforce, in the language a model follows, so the model stops producing what the gates reject.
